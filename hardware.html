Supported hardware

MockTab supports Wacom USB and Bluetooth tablets from 1996 to today, plus some Xencelabs tablets. Each table shows pressure, buttons, connection, and whether the model has been tested.

What the symbols mean

✓Works
Tested on a real tablet.
≈Likely works
A closely related model already works, but this model may need testing.
…Untested
Possibly works, but needs hands-on verification. Please try it and report how it goes.

Product IDs are USB IDs; Bluetooth and dongle versions are listed under their USB entry. Tablet not here? Choose Help › Collect Device Data… and send us the zip file.

Release years for some models come from TheSevenPens’ DrawTabData.

Quick Keys’ 8 keys, mode button, and dial work the same plugged in or through the wireless dongle. MockTab can’t tell the bundle’s 3-button pen from the thinner 2-button pen, so both appear as “Xencelabs Pen.”

Wireless

  • USB dongle (ACK-40401, ID 0x0084): works once paired with a supported tablet.
  • Bluetooth: works on the Intuos Pro Gen 2 (PTH-460/660/860) and Gen 3 (PTK-470/670/870); the Intuos CTL-4100WL/6100WL and Wacom One S/M are less tested. Pair in System Settings; MockTab lists the tablet under its USB entry.
  • Wireless kit (Intuos4, Intuos5, and Intuos Pro Gen 1; receiver IDs 0x009D and 0x009A): works on an Intuos5 L (PTH-850); other models are untested.
  • Second Bluetooth device (Intuos Pro Gen 2): Wacom’s paper-notes feature uses it, not tablet input. Pair the one whose name ends in “BT.”
  • ExpressKey Remote (EKR-100, PID 0x0331): its 17 keys and touch ring work through the remote's own USB receiver. MockTab follows the ring mode the remote selects, but can't change it.

Not supported

  • Serial and ADB-era Wacom tablets, including the serial PL-series displays. MockTab does support the USB Cintiq 21UX (0x003F) and 21UX2 (0x00CC).
  • Wacom STU signature pads (STU-430, STU-500, STU-520, STU-530, STU-540, and similar), which use a different protocol.
  • Bluetooth tablets that act as a serial port (CTE-630BT and XD-0608-BT).
  • macOS older than 13 (Ventura).

Have a tablet that isn’t listed, or tried one marked untested? Please open an issue with the model number and what happened, and attach the zip file from Help › Collect Device Data….
