Supported hardware

MockTab supports Wacom USB and Bluetooth tablets from the early 2000s to about 2020: nearly a hundred models across a dozen generations. A few newer models are still experimental. MockTab also supports some Xencelabs tablets. Each table lists pressure range, buttons, connection, and test status.

Status legend

  • ✓ Confirmed: works on real hardware.
  • ⚠ Generic-decoder: the family's decoder, built from the Linux driver and OpenTabletDriver, covers it, but this model needs further testing.
  • … Pending: MockTab has a decoder for it, but it needs testing on real hardware. It may work; please report results.

Product IDs are USB IDs. Bluetooth and wireless-dongle versions have their own IDs, but MockTab merges them into the USB entry, so each tablet appears once. For a tablet missing here, choose Help › Collect Device Data… and send the zip file it makes.

Release details from some models come from TheSevenPens’ DrawTabData.

PenPartner & Graphire · 1996–2007

Compact consumer line using the 8-byte Graphire report. No tilt, rotation, or ExpressKeys, though a couple of Bamboo-branded models added a few buttons. Pressure tops out at 511 (9-bit); the original PenPartner tops out at 255. The decoder follows the Linux driver. A Bamboo Fun (CTE-650) capture confirmed the pen, but these models need further testing.

Cintiq, Wacom One & MobileStudio pen displays · 2005–present

The 24HD has a touch ring on each side, each with three assignable modes. On models with finger touch, MockTab pairs the separate touch sensor with its display automatically, so it has no row of its own.

Quick Keys' 8 keys, mode button, and dial work the same directly or through the wireless dongle; MockTab treats both as one device. Reports don't distinguish the bundle's 3-button pen from the thinner 2-button pen, so both appear as “Xencelabs Pen.”

Wireless

  • USB dongle (ACK-40401, PID 0x0084): works once paired. MockTab detects the paired tablet's format on connection.
  • Bluetooth: works on the Intuos Pro Gen 2 (PTH-460/660/860) and Gen 3 (PTK-470/670/870). The Intuos CTL-4100WL/6100WL and Wacom One S/M also connect over Bluetooth, with less testing. Pair in System Settings; MockTab finds them and merges them with the matching USB entry.
  • Wireless receivers (Intuos4/5/Pro Gen 1 WL): the receivers (IDs 0x009D and 0x009A) pass along the paired tablet's reports. Pen input works on an Intuos5 L (PTH-850) over the wireless kit; other models need further testing.
  • Intuos Pro Gen 2 “LE” identity: these tablets also show a second Bluetooth device, which Wacom's paper notes mode uses, not tablet input. Pair the “BT” one instead.
  • ExpressKey Remote (EKR-100, PID 0x0331): its 17 keys and touch ring work through the remote's own USB receiver. MockTab follows the ring mode the remote selects, but can't change it.

Not supported

  • Serial and ADB-era Wacom tablets, including the serial PL-series displays. MockTab does support the USB Cintiq 21UX (0x003F) and 21UX2 (0x00CC).
  • Wacom STU signature pads (STU-430, STU-500, STU-520, STU-530, STU-540, and similar), which use a different protocol.
  • Bluetooth tablets that act as a serial port (CTE-630BT and XD-0608-BT).
  • macOS older than 13 (Ventura).
